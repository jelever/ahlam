Ahlam Alqamar: brand sheet

Logo, colours, type and web icons for شركة أحلام القمر للتجارة. All logo files are in dist/logo/, as SVG (for print and the web) and PNG at 1x and 2x.

Status: a proposal for the client's approval. The mark is a clean redraw of the "Warm Home Collections" emblem the owner chose on 2026-11-04: a house with a chimney holding a floor lamp, an armchair and a potted plant. It has no crescent or moon, as the client's brief asks.

Logo

Mark: full and small

Use the full mark from 32 px up. Below that, and for app and browser icons, use the small mark (the house and the armchair only): the lamp and plant cannot be read at 16 px.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin of at least a quarter of the mark's height on every side. Minimum sizes: the mark 24 px (6 mm in print), a horizontal lockup 120 px wide (30 mm).
