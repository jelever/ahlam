Ahlam Alqamar: brand sheet

Logo, slogan, colours, type and web icons for شركة أحلام القمر للتجارة. All logo files are in dist/logo/, as SVG (for print and the web) and PNG at 1x and 2x.

The logo is the company name, set in Alexandria and Figtree, with no symbol (owner's decision, 2026-11-05). Where the name cannot fit, in browser tabs and app icons, the monogram أق (the initials of أحلام القمر) stands in.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin of at least the height of the name's letters on every side. Minimum widths: the Arabic wordmark 110 px (28 mm in print), the English 140 px (36 mm). Below that, use the monogram.
